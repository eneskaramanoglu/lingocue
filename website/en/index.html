Features

All of subtitling in one app

From recording to release: transcription, correction, translation, styling and export. Built for Turkish first, with its casing and spelling rules, and useful for many other languages.

Speech to text

Whisper runs free on your own computer and graphics card, with word-level timing. Gemini Transcribe is there for noisy recordings.

AI correction

Spelling, punctuation and names get fixed while lines stay readable. Names in your glossary are spelled the same everywhere.

Several languages at once

English, Korean, German, Japanese and more; each language becomes its own track. Korean follows Netflix's reading rules.

Your style

Fonts and colors, a block box, line boxes or a full-width band, frames, karaoke and bilingual subtitles. Templates apply it in one click.

Preview equals output

What you see in the preview is exactly what gets burned in. Fast export on NVIDIA, AMD, Intel or Apple hardware; SRT, VTT and ASS too.

Local and private if you want

With Whisper and Ollama everything stays on your computer: no internet, no account. Cloud engines are used only when you choose them.

How it works

Four steps to a subtitled video

The app's top bar shows the order of work. Each step makes its own track, so the original is never lost.

Transcribe

Drop in a video. Speech becomes word-timed text, split into readable lines; doubtful lines are flagged.

Correct

AI fixes spelling and punctuation. You see every changed line and can go back to the original.

Translate

Pick one or more languages. The video's context is read once, so terms and tone stay consistent.

Export

Burn the subtitles in, add them as a separate track, or save a subtitle file.

Style

Subtitles that match your brand

Set the background box color, opacity and padding, add a frame; the preview updates instantly.

  • Block box, line boxes or a full-width band
  • Karaoke: words light up as they are spoken (Shorts style)
  • Bilingual mode: the translation under the main subtitle
  • Project fonts, with a warning for missing Turkish or Korean letters
Translation

Many languages in one click

Choose the target languages and see the cost up front. Each language becomes its own track with the exact same timing.

  • The video's summary, tone and speakers are read first
  • Glossary terms and locked lines are kept
  • Unfinished lines are retried without paying again for finished ones
  • Works with a free Gemini key or a local model (Ollama)

Download

LingoCue 1.0.0

Free and open source (MIT). Updates arrive inside the app.

Windows 10 / 11

Installs without admin rights and updates itself.

Download installer (.exe)

If you see “Windows protected your PC”: More info → Run anyway (the app is not signed with a paid certificate). To try it without installing: portable zip.

macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon

Macs with M1, M2, M3 and M4 chips. The GPU is used through Metal.

Download disk image (.dmg)

Drag LingoCue to Applications. The app is not notarized by Apple, so run this once in Terminal before the first start:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LingoCue.app

FFmpeg (needed on both)

Audio and video are processed with FFmpeg; install it once:

winget install Gyan.FFmpeg
brew install ffmpeg-full

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. LingoCue is open source under the MIT license. The local engines (Whisper, Ollama) are completely free; if you use Gemini, Google charges for what you use and the app shows an estimate before each run. There is a setting for free Gemini keys.

Is the interface available in English?

Not yet: the app is in Turkish. Transcription works in dozens of languages, and translation goes into English, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Arabic, Russian, Japanese and Azerbaijani.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not with the local engines: the Whisper model downloads once (~1 GB) on first use, then everything works offline. Gemini needs a connection.

What if I have no graphics card, or a weak one?

Whisper also runs on the CPU, just slower. The app detects your graphics card and suggests a model and settings that fit; a 4 GB card is plenty.

Does it run on Intel Macs?

Not for now: the Mac version is for Apple Silicon (M1–M4).

How do updates work?

On Windows, the installed version downloads updates in the background and offers “restart and update”. On a Mac the app tells you about a new version and opens the download page.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

In the app, Yardım → Tanılama paketi oluştur saves a zip to the desktop; then Yardım → Hata bildir opens a new issue on GitHub where you can attach it. Nothing is sent automatically, and your keys are never in the package.
